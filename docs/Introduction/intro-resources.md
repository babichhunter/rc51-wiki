# Resources Used in the RC51 ECU Project

Below is a list of websites and platforms that were instrumental in helping me research, source components, and learn techniques for this build. These include educational platforms, forums, suppliers, and personal documentation.

## [FuelTech](https://www.fueltech.net/)

Manufacturer of high‑performance ECUs and engine management systems. I used their FT550 unit for this build, and their site includes product specs, wiring guides, and tuning software.

## [High Performance Academy (HPA)](https://www.hpacademy.com/)

Online training platform offering courses on EFI tuning, wiring, and engine building. Their wiring fundamentals and motorsport harness courses were especially helpful.  
Recommended courses:

- Wiring Fundamentals  
- Practical Wiring  
- EFI Tuning Fundamentals  
- Practical Standalone Tuning  

## [RC51 Forums](https://www.rc51forums.com/)

Community forum dedicated to Honda RC51 owners. Great place to find legacy knowledge, troubleshooting tips, and real‑world experience from other RC owners.

## [ProWire USA](https://www.prowireusa.com/)

Supplier of motorsport‑grade wiring, connectors, and tools. I sourced TXL wire and Deutsch connectors from here.

## [Corsa Technic](https://www.corsa-technic.com/)

Specialized supplier of OEM and hard‑to‑find connectors and terminals. Their catalog is great for matching factory‑style plugs and sourcing small quantities.

## [TE Connectivity](https://www.te.com/en/home.html)

Manufacturer of high‑quality connectors and sensors. I referenced their datasheets and product specs when selecting components.

## [Motorcycle Terminal (Cycle Terminal)](https://www.cycleterminal.com/)

Supplier of motorcycle‑specific terminals, connectors, and wiring kits.
